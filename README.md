## Projects

The projects below represent the kind of software I enjoy building: useful products with thoughtful interfaces, practical AI applications, and systems designed around real user problems.

### FinWise AI

An AI-driven financial literacy platform that combines a money-personality quiz, an adaptive AI mentor, and risk-free story-based market simulations. It is designed to make financial concepts easier to understand through personalized and interactive learning.

`Next.js` `AI Mentor` `Behavioral Finance` `Market Simulations`

[Live Demo](https://getfinwise.vercel.app/) · [Code](https://github.com/parteek1907/Finwise-AI)

### Ares

A local-first, privacy-focused AI memory assistant. Ares works with PDFs, notes, browser history, local embeddings, and screen OCR to consolidate personal context into useful daily summaries without relying on a cloud-first workflow.

`Tauri` `React` `Rust` `SQLite`

[Code](https://github.com/adityaa6060/Ares)

### Zir0

A borderless payments platform that intelligently routes transfers across traditional and decentralized rails. Zir0 focuses on scam detection, address-poisoning protection, and human-readable explanations for financial transactions.

`Fintech` `Smart Routing` `Payment Protection`

[Live Demo](https://zir0.vercel.app/) · [Code](https://github.com/parteek1907/Ziro)
